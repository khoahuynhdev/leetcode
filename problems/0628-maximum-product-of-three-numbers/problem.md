# 0628. Maximum Product of Three Numbers

Given an integer array `nums`, _find three numbers whose product is maximum and return the maximum product_.

 

**Example 1:**
    
    
    **Input:** nums = [1,2,3]
    **Output:** 6
    

**Example 2:**
    
    
    **Input:** nums = [1,2,3,4]
    **Output:** 24
    

**Example 3:**
    
    
    **Input:** nums = [-1,-2,-3]
    **Output:** -6
    

 

**Constraints:**

  * `3 <= nums.length <= 104`
  * `-1000 <= nums[i] <= 1000`
